Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This policy is a general website template and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before launch, particularly after analytics, form, hosting or marketing services are selected.

Winzosoft Pvt. Ltd. (“Winzosoft”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects the privacy of website visitors and business contacts. This policy explains the types of information that may be collected through this website and how that information may be used.

Information you provide

You may choose to provide information when contacting us, including your name, business email, phone number, company name, project requirements, estimated budget and other details included in your message. Please do not send passwords, payment card information or other unnecessary sensitive data through an inquiry form.

Information collected automatically

The current static website does not intentionally store inquiry submissions or use analytics. If analytics, cookies, server logs or third-party form services are added before launch, this policy and any consent notices should be updated to explain the data collected, purposes and choices available to visitors.

How information may be used

  • To respond to inquiries and arrange project discussions.
  • To understand business requirements and prepare relevant proposals.
  • To provide requested services and ongoing support.
  • To maintain website security and diagnose technical issues.
  • To meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, communicate with you or deliver agreed services. Such providers should only process information for the specified purpose and under appropriate safeguards.

International communication

Winzosoft is based in India and may work with clients and service providers in other countries. Where information is transferred internationally, reasonable contractual, technical and organizational safeguards should be considered in light of applicable requirements.

Data retention

Business inquiry information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a potential or active business relationship, maintain appropriate records and satisfy legal obligations.

Security

We aim to apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure, so visitors should limit submissions to information necessary for the inquiry.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection to certain processing. To make a privacy request, contact Winzosoft using the phone number or business contact details below. We may need to verify the request before responding.

Third-party links

This website may link to external services such as WhatsApp or social networks. Their privacy practices are controlled by their respective providers, and visitors should review those policies separately.

Children’s privacy

This business website is not directed to children and is not intended to collect personal information from children.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when website features, service providers or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will indicate the latest revision.
